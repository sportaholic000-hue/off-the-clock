# Comparison pages: buying-question research — 2026-10-01

Scope: the 12 existing guides on growth/public-pages-review @ 2a6dfd3, with five buying questions and a low-effort conversation CTA. No competitor account, paid service, call or live integration was tested. This is an original source comparison and fit assessment, not a first-hand performance review.

## Findings that changed the implementation

- The main action asked visitors to prepare a job brief. That required scope and measurements before visitors could explore the product. The owner prefers a product conversation with no price book or account prerequisite.
- Booking and website inquiries are not unique to Off The Clock. Ruby documents calendar integrations and paid chat plans; Podium lists dispatch booking and website chat; Avoca advertises web-lead handling; Housecall Pro documents CSR AI Chat booking. The new answers credit those capabilities.
- Podium advertises price-book access; Housecall Pro CSR AI reads configured service prices; ServiceTitan documents dispatch fees. None of those statements alone resolves whether a measured multi-item project is calculated. The pages request an appropriate demonstration instead of claiming competitors cannot quote.
- A Jobber Receptionist add-on price is not the total Jobber subscription. Human Smith.ai booking can have additional fees. ServiceTitan's per-call figure is not an all-in account or phone bill. Allowances use different units; no automatic minutes-to-calls equivalence, currency conversion or savings claim is made.
- AnswerConnect's currently retrieved Canadian pricing destination did not expose numerical plans. Dialzara pricing retrieval failed; indexed material was old/inconsistent. Their former numerical examples were removed, not redated as verified current prices.
- ServiceTitan's official public offer was search-indexed at $2.75/call, with a 12-month rate lock and monthly usage billing. Direct parsed body was incomplete. The settings article exceeded the reader size limit; official indexed settings and linked release notes establish narrower dispatch/routing claims. Those limits remain visible.
- The owner confirmed on October 1 that Opus has not begun live-demo completion yet. No working voice URL is configured. The comparison preview never starts a microphone or provider session.

## Evidence boundaries

Off The Clock's monthly offer is locked: Operator $119 / 300 minutes; QuoteDone $279 / 1,200 minutes; $0.35/minute extra usage; no setup fee. Scale is not offered. Billing currency, tax/phone charges and live quote-to-phone/calendar acceptance need confirmation before publication. These pages do not certify the voice runtime, quote engine or launch readiness.

Google's review guidance recommends original research, buyer-relevant decision factors, supporting evidence and an explanation of benefits and drawbacks. Sources support the advertised capabilities here; UI tests support only this site's behavior. No third-party test score, testimonial, conversion increase or ranking guarantee is invented.
