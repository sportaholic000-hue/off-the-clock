See the calculation rules

Each quantity × unit cost is calculated from exact decimal inputs and rounded once to cents. A positive half-cent rounds up. Rounded line totals are added. Selling prices entered in “Check my price” must resolve to whole cents.

In “Set my markup”, price = entered costs × (1 + markup ÷ 100), rounded up to the first whole cent that meets the entered markup. The displayed achieved percentage can differ slightly because money uses whole cents. With zero costs, markup is undefined; the formula returns $0, not a pricing recommendation.

Amount left = price − entered costs. Markup = amount left ÷ costs. Margin = amount left ÷ price. Percentages are displayed to two decimal places, not reused to calculate money. A zero denominator is N/A. Missing values block results, rather than being treated as zero.

These figures are not accounting net or gross profit. Unentered expenses and tax obligations are not calculated. USD/CAD are denomination labels only: changing the label does not convert amounts.
