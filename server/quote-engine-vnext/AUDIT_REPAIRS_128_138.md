## Current explicit contracts

- Persisted services require a valid `id` UUID and explicit `source` (`MANUAL`, `AI_SUGGESTED`, or `AI_INTERVIEW`). `origin` is the creation receipt with exactly `serviceId`, `source`, `ownerId`, `operationId`, and `createdAt`. Its service and source must match. Ordinary `editVNextService` operations cannot set identity, source, origin, or approval receipts; approval changes use the separate approval operation. Missing provenance reviews; there is no deletion-to-manual fallback and no manual-replacement operation in the candidate.
- AI value approvals retain exactly `value`, `serviceType`, `serviceId`, `ownerId`, `operationId`, and `approvedAt`. The exact current value, persisted UUID, and creation-receipt owner must match. This also covers present `knownOfferings` and `zeroPricePolicy`.
- **Trust boundary:** these pure helpers validate bindings, not authentication. A future trusted adapter must authenticate/authorize the owner, load origin from protected persistence, persist updates atomically, and prevent clients from replacing the trusted receipt. A hostile caller allowed to replace the entire service and receipt can forge self-asserted metadata. The isolated candidate does not claim to solve that persistence/authentication problem. Any deliberate manual replacement requires a separate authenticated, audited creation with a new identity; that integration is not implemented here.
- Every used open `slug` price selector requires `confirmedFacts[field] = {status: 'identified', offeringId: '<UUID>'}`, matching `knownOfferings[field][selectedValue]`. The owner registry is explicit, separate from pricing. The engine never derives known offerings or real customer confirmations from price-map keys. `existingFloorType=none` is the explicit absence of existing floor scope. Missing registries retain an exact owner diagnostic; missing/contradictory confirmations require verification. Existing explicit unknown-scope gates remain additional checks.
- Activation uses clearly synthetic confirmations only for explicitly registered offerings to test configured pricing. These probes establish no facts about a real project.
- Required core labor, material, removal, preparation, and other indispensable selected rates cannot be unclassified zero. Optional charges, disposal, zero minima, percentages, and no physical scope keep their defined zero behavior. No price or scope is inferred.
- Optional `zeroPricePolicy` has exactly `serviceId`, `ownerId`, `operationId`, `approvedAt`, `freeCompleteService` (boolean for the untiered offering), `freeTiers` (explicit configured tier names), and `includedPrices` (zero-price path to the positive required price path that includes it). Its audited identity must match. Inclusion is accepted only when the positive covering price is required for this selected scope; a minimum or dormant charge cannot cover a missing core price. The policy never rewrites a price or suppresses an existing charge. A free offering containing a positive calculated charge reviews; an entire zero total without the explicit free designation reviews. Each tier is checked independently.
- Every internal ready root, option record, root calculation record, review, and lead carries the current build identifier. Persisted service identity is retained internally. Customer output continues to exclude identities, origin, approvals, raw rates, markup/margin, and internal evidence.
- Internal range endpoints remain integer cents. Customer ranges use low=floor(cents/100), high=ceil(cents/100), and midpoint=nearest whole dollar. Endpoints expand outward. Only a genuinely single-price result with zero configured buffer and equal exact endpoints may display cents. No global nearest-$10 rule applies. Existing monetary minimum and buffer arithmetic is unchanged.
- Margin mode uses the active persisted field `markupPercent`: all finite `0 <= markupPercent < 100` values are allowed if calculated money is safe. There is no additional product maximum. This is settled, not an unresolved owner decision.
- Rational-to-number projections now always round directly from the rational, nearest/ties-to-even, including subnormals. Exact rational arithmetic and cent rounding remain authoritative.
