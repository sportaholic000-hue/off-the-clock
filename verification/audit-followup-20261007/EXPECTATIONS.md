# Follow-up repair expectations, recorded before execution

All examples are synthetic. No formula, stored price or live record changes.

- A saved interview price of $0.03 survives an older tab's attempt to save
  $0.04, whether its revision is stale or missing. The owner keeps the unsaved
  answer, reviews the newer draft, and explicitly confirms before another save.
- Mulch area means square feet; directly entered mulch volume means cubic
  yards. Changing that choice clears the previous quantity so 300 square feet
  cannot silently become 300 cubic yards. Unrelated edits keep the quantity.
- With material $45/yard, installation $30/yard and no fees, tax, markup or
  minimum: 300 square feet at 3 inches = 300*3/324 yards. The 15% material
  ordering allowance gives $143.75 material and $83.33 labor = $227.08.
  Directly stated 300 yards has no ordering allowance: 300*(45+30) = $22,500.
  These existing amounts must remain unchanged; displayed units must differ.
- Website draft "$20" retains an adjacent "Friday and Saturday only";
  "$100.00 each" retains "Minimum 10 items per order". Separate offers keep
  separate conditions. Bounded plain-text paragraphs retain their conditions.
- An oversized price-and-condition block is omitted and disclosed as partial
  coverage, rather than emitting its price without the condition.

Phone scope confirmation and CSS stylesheet visibility are separate findings;
this batch does not claim to fix them.
