## Sources without direct numeric support

Legacy pitch/story/access/slope/room multipliers and many waste defaults have **no primary citation stored with their definitions**. The engine spec expressly calls these midpoints rather than universal truths. Specific October3 owner amendments approve some exact values and component rules; those are owner policy evidence, not empirical estimates. Source availability does not turn a policy into a demonstrated measurement.

No generic compaction allowance, topsoil depth, product coverage, roll width, carton size, pallet area, bundle count or short-load threshold was invented by the production paths reviewed. Help examples and the hypothetical cases here must not be promoted into default owner rates.
