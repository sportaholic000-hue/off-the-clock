## Confirmation and source boundaries

`inventory.json` covers162 numeric Class2/proposal leaves:134 automatic numeric leaves plus28 optional-only leaves. Eight of the automatic roofing leaves also have starting provenance, making36 starting leaves in total across five service types. All20 types are listed, including mowing/custom with zero Class2 leaves. `starting-state-evidence.json` demonstrates legacy, new, adopted, individually confirmed and owner-edited states using synthetic records. It is **not** a census of actual owners or a claim any real business adopted these factors.

Normal service approval and individual starting-value confirmation are different. The automatic map fills a missing root and is displayed for approval; it does not create an independent citation/confirmation receipt for each legacy factor. Optional proposals remain pending for old services until adoption. Pre-existing owner numbers stay unchanged. These mechanics support informed owner acceptance; they do not validate a number statistically.
