This is a report of a production-mode server and built client driven by Chromium against a synthetic HTTPS origin. It is not a deployment or a test with a real provider. ## Failed or blocked steps

- **1 — failed/blocked:** Production `EMAIL_PROVIDER=console` accepts registration (`202`) and says “Check your email,” but sends no console message or link. Fake Resend delivery let both accounts finish verification and session lifecycle.
- **5 — blocked without a fixture:** The owner saved “About & area,” but booking configuration still reported `serviceArea.configured=false`; the owner UI had no structured service-area entry. A synthetic structured area and fake Google connection were seeded to complete the customer quote/booking exercise.
- **6 — failed:** Three widget quotes and one confirmed booking appeared in Dashboard, Quotes and Calendar, while Leads showed “No saved leads yet.”
- **7 — blocked for the full seat journey:** There was no owner-facing staff creation/invitation flow exercised; the staff account was seeded. Staff/other-owner isolation checks passed, but billing-role enforcement could not be judged with the billing status route absent.
- **8 — partially failed/intermittent:** Signed fake voice quote and 125-second completion reached the same database and billed three minutes. In one preserved run, subsequent owner Calls/Leads/Quotes requests returned `503 SESSION_STORE_UNAVAILABLE`; a repeat instead raised `SqliteError: disk I/O error` opening a second synthetic database connection. This is not proof of the cause in production. Real model speech/audio was not exercised.
- **9 — blocked by the prescribed no-Stripe environment:** Billing showed “Billing is not configured yet,” and `GET /api/billing/status` returned `404 {"error":"Not found"}`. Trial details and the post-call minute meter could not be checked on that page.

## Setup and observations

`npm run build` passed; [raw build output](raw/build.log). The server was `server/src/server.js` with `NODE_ENV=production` and a fresh SQLite volume per run. The browser was Playwright Chromium (`.browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome`) on Node `v22.23.3`; its synthetic `https://synthetic-app.example.invalid` browser origin was proxied to localhost. The widget ran on the separate `https://synthetic-widget.example.invalid` origin. Source files were not changed. The all-local fake boundaries were:

- Google Calendar: existing `test/fixtures/bookingCalendarProvider20261006.mjs`, loaded by [fakeProviders.mjs](fakeProviders.mjs). Its fake free/busy event was Tuesday October 13, 10:00–11:00 Halifax, and it accepted the synthetic event insertion.
- Account email: the fake Resend `fetch`/accepted-ID shape from `test/accountEmail.spec.mjs`, with messages confined to `@example.invalid` in a local file. The initial `EMAIL_PROVIDER=console` attempt was kept as a separate failing observation. No real email was sent.
- Voice: fake `googleClient.live.connect`/callbacks/tool responses and signed Twilio inbound, media WebSocket and status-callback shape from `test/voiceQuotePathCases20261005.mjs`. The Google fake emitted transcription/tool calls, not generated speech; the Twilio signature used a synthetic token. No real Twilio, Gemini, Google, Stripe, email or SMS account was used.
- Trial evidence, the staff user, structured service area and encrypted fake calendar credential were local synthetic database fixtures. Those actions were **not** owner UI successes. Trial evidence was necessary because a real card/Stripe checkout was forbidden. The fake calendar/area were necessary for direct booking. These dependencies narrow the end-to-end claim.

The first recorded full run got through the staff checks and the signed voice/callback, then the owner screen requests returned 503 ([raw JSON](raw/execution-intermittent-auth.json), [server log](raw/server-intermittent-auth.log), [screen](screenshots/step-8-intermittent-auth.png)). The same script was repeated and encountered a local SQLite `disk I/O error` while preparing the synthetic staff row; after shutdown, that volume's `PRAGMA integrity_check` returned `ok`. The repeat's observations and stack traces are in [raw/execution.json](raw/execution.json). A prior successful voice-screen pass was seen while developing the harness, but its raw JSON was overwritten; it is **not counted** as a final pass. The final report relies on the preserved failing runs.
