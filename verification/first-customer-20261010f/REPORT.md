# Synthetic first-customer rehearsal — release candidate f

Base: `claude/release-candidate-20261010f` at `0069c801c37e98f052c4e6d237437259a4024e91`. Branch: `verify/first-customer-20261010f`. [Expected results and handwritten money](EXPECTED.md) were written before the corresponding requests. The final [runner](rehearsal.mjs) used the built client, the production Node server, a real Playwright Chromium browser and signed synthetic voice traffic. [Raw browser/API observations](raw/execution.json), [server log before a controlled restart](raw/server-before-starter.log), [server log after it](raw/server.log), and [build log](build.log) are retained. The controlled restart let a trial fixture be inserted with the server closed. All identities, numbers, domains, job facts and provider credentials were synthetic. No message, charge or provider request left the fake boundary.

## Failed or blocked steps

- **Step 1, console delivery:** `POST /api/auth/register` returned `202 {"ok":true}` and the screen promised “Check your email to verify your account,” but `EMAIL_PROVIDER=console` wrote no verification link or `[email:console]` marker. This path cannot complete the verification step. The existing fake Resend delivery then verified both accounts and the session lifecycle passed.
- **Step 9, Billing:** Under the required no-Stripe setting, `GET /api/billing/status` returned `404 {"error":"Not found"}`. Billing showed “Billing is not configured yet,” while the page also offered “Choose a subscription or manage your existing billing” and “Refresh billing status.” The trial details and pending-duration indication could not be checked on Billing. This is an environment-bound block; it does not establish behavior with live Stripe configured.

The final runner recorded **21 checks: 19 pass, 2 fail**. The failed checks are the two above. A `POST /api/pricebook/preview` request returned `409 {"error":"Save this service before previewing it."}` while a new service was unsaved; the editor still saved and approved it, and the first visit showed no false “This price book changed” dialog. Browser `net::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID` messages were caused by the synthetic `.invalid` HTTPS origins used for the local browser proxy. The expected `503 TEXT_AI_UNAVAILABLE` is covered in step 2.

## Synthetic fixtures still used and why

1. **Email:** fake Resend `fetch` response and accepted-ID capture modeled on `test/accountEmail.spec.mjs` in [fakeProviders.mjs](fakeProviders.mjs). `EMAIL_PROVIDER=console` was probed first and failed as above. No email left local storage.
2. **Calendar:** `test/fixtures/bookingCalendarProvider20261006.mjs` intercepted the Google free/busy/event calls. A local encrypted `primary` calendar connection and one Oct 13 busy event use the schema pattern from `test/fixtures/bookingCalendarApplication20261006.mjs`. Real OAuth would violate the no-Google-account rule; the service area was entered **on screen**.
3. **Trial:** synthetic `billingAccounts`, subscription evidence and trialing status were inserted for Operator, QuoteDone and Starter using the existing test evidence shape. A real trial/card needs Stripe, which was forbidden. The late Starter fixture was inserted during a controlled local server restart to avoid competing SQLite connections.
4. **Phone number:** local provisioned `businessProfiles` numbers were seeded for the three plans because purchasing a real Twilio number was forbidden. The forwarding receipt itself came from a **signed synthetic inbound webhook** and was read on the actual Onboarding setup screen; it was not seeded.
5. **Voice:** fake `googleClient.live.connect`/transcription/tool callbacks and signed Twilio inbound/media WebSocket/status callbacks follow `test/voiceQuotePathCases20261005.mjs`. They exercised the actual voice runtime and database, without provider speech/audio or a real phone.
6. **Website:** Playwright served a plain HTML page at an allowed synthetic HTTPS origin and loaded the product’s built `widget.js`. This was a browser page, not a database quote fixture.

## Not checked

Live carrier forwarding and no-answer delay, real Twilio number purchase, actual Gemini speech and 12-minute call continuity, Google OAuth and a real calendar account, inbox delivery, real Stripe checkout/charge, paid Starter 150-minute metering, real SMS (none sent), 90-day erasure after time passes, Railway bucket backups, deploys and provider reliability were outside the synthetic/no-deploy boundary. The no-Stripe Billing 404 blocks observing its pending-duration UI. The headless browser’s synthetic HTTPS certificate errors do not establish a production certificate fault.
