# Off The Clock — read-only launch inspection, September 30, 2026

**The repository is not ready to launch the complete product yet.** Accurate quoting from approved owner price books has made substantial progress, and the website quote-to-booking path works in the independently checked normal cases. The production phone entry point, truthful carrier switching, customer fulfillment and a remaining booking recovery gap still prevent calling this a proven phone-and-widget operator.

The intended product remains an AI operator that quotes sufficiently measured supported jobs from the business owner's approved prices and books them into the owner's calendar, serving Canada and the USA. Review or site-visit outcomes belong to genuinely insufficient or out-of-scope work. This review does not recommend reducing the product to a receptionist.

**Tax finding and its current status.** I reproduced a silent owner-setting overwrite on e2ccbda: Alaska TAX_ALL 5% saved as TAX_NONE 0%; Nova Scotia 13% saved as 14%, and a submitted no-tax choice also saved as 14%. New York 8.875% was preserved. The defect was shared with the engine agent, who independently reproduced it in the onboarding UI and saved correction 4aaded3. I checked the changed source and reran my four API probes against its exact server entry bytes and unchanged backend dependencies: **4/4 pass**. The save-route overwrite is therefore **fixed in that reviewed commit**, rather than an open launch blocker. Original failures and the correction results are both retained in tax-results.json and tax-fixed-results.json.

The original tests were owner-input authority probes, not recommendations for lawful tax rates. Alaska cannot safely be treated as universally untaxed merely because it lacks a statewide sales tax; its municipalities can impose sales taxes, as explained by the [Alaska state assessor](https://www.commerce.alaska.gov/web/dcra/OfficeoftheStateAssessor/AlaskaSalesTaxInformation.aspx). Tax lookup should offer a prefill and validate explicit owner settings, never silently replace an entered choice.

One UI follow-up remains: Canadian onboarding exposes a combined rate and requires it to be positive, while the API and later book settings support no-tax mode. Review whether every intended owner can express the approved configuration during onboarding. This is separate from the corrected save-route defect.

**Other unfinished work and product improvements.**

- Leads, quotes and calendar controls are real implemented surfaces. The full Calls/Customers/admin management promised in the broader spec is not mounted as a completed product. Production dashboard call activity is not populated. Owner troubleshooting and visibility into pending/failed calls and bookings should precede extensive cosmetic work.
- The public /quote/:publicKey route is service intake. It is not the promised individual issued-quote page with signed acceptance, PDF/export and change/reschedule/cancel actions. Those promises need the actual customer artifact and lifecycle.
- Canada/USA currency is underspecified: no explicit USD/CAD field was found in the selected engine/application sources, and the starter-price prompt asks for US dollars even for a Canadian business. Define owner currency and show it consistently in quotes and plan prices. This is an ambiguity and rate-seeding concern, not proof of an incorrect currency conversion.
- Owner price-book files use an atomic temporary-write/rename pattern, which is useful. It is not a backup/restore strategy. A deployment must preserve the approved book revision and database together across restarts and recovery.
- Health currently reports ok rather than proving database, calendar or voice readiness. The deployed reverse-proxy/IP configuration, operational alerts, onboarding recovery and cancellation/offboarding routing need acceptance.
- The public homepage is still represented by a design-reference prototype. The committed growth content and sales demo are separate implementations. Verified production signup/demo wiring, current claims, support and customer-facing data/retention explanations still need to be assembled.
- Account recovery email delivery remains an explicit later owner gate: real sender/domain and inbox delivery were not tested here. This review does not ask the owner to set up business email now.

**Best opportunities to improve the product and win customers, after the core launch gates pass.**

1. **Turn the existing job brief into a real owner-price-book demonstration.** Let a prospect bring one actual job and approved prices. Show the measured inputs, issued customer price and confirmed booking, with a clear explanation when more information is needed. Connect the current CTA to a usable signup or demonstration handoff. Track brief completion, demonstration completion and trials rather than assuming page views produce customers.
2. **Build proof around the strongest supported trades.** Start content with fencing, painting and flooring examples that the released product can actually reproduce. Show supplied measurements, inclusion/exclusion boundaries, owner approval and calendar outcomes. Obtain permission for real customer stories and use measured results. Do not invent testimonials, claimed savings or universal trade coverage.
3. **Publish the comparison library with working conversion and accurate claims.** Set the verified host, canonical URLs and sitemap; remove noindex only when ready; connect every high-intent page to the demonstration/signup journey. Google recommends useful, original content and [evidence-based reviews](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews). Its [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) make large volumes of repetitive doorway pages a poor shortcut.
4. **Prepare a website-agency/referral kit for the widget.** Agencies already maintain service-business websites. A tested installation guide, owner approval workflow, visible quote accuracy example and clear referral arrangement could give them a concrete reason to introduce customers. No outreach was sent.
5. **Do founder-led walkthroughs with the first ten prospective owners.** Use their actual books and representative jobs to uncover import, unit and scope friction. Record approved quote accuracy, successful bookings, owner intervention and onboarding time. Use that evidence to improve the product and support credible case studies. This does not redefine the launch as a reduced receptionist product.

For the product itself, the most useful near-term additions are a visible readiness check that completes one real quote-to-calendar journey before activation, actionable pending/failure recovery for owners, explicit currency, and stronger price-book import/unit guidance. Extensive new pages, cosmetic dashboard work and speculative features should follow the complete operator path.

**Required evidence before declaring launch-ready.** Agree one exact release commit containing the approved engine/calendar work, auth fixes and completed voice/routing implementation. Prove representative owner-approved jobs through both phone and widget, confirm the actual calendar event and customer notification, exercise ON/OFF with the owner's normal phone handling, rerun restart/retry boundaries, verify billing fulfillment, and restore the persistent database and price books. Those gates match the intended product; passing an isolated engine or a build does not replace them.
