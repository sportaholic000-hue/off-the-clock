# Growth proposal follow-up: immediate voice demonstration

Owner steering: asking a prospect for approved prices before they try Off The Clock takes too long, and many will not have their book available. The first Off The Clock prototype used a voice demonstration that explained what the product could do for the visitor. Preserve that direction.

This proposal supersedes the audit's suggestion to make an owner-price-book job challenge the first acquisition CTA. Actual approved pricing belongs in customer onboarding and later accuracy verification. No product source was changed, no marketing was published, and no outreach was sent.

**Recommended journey:** relevant page → Talk to Off The Clock → contextual voice conversation → Get started, with a walkthrough option for owners who need help.

Use one shared sales demo with a relevant introduction and discussion based on the page the visitor came from. Let them begin without uploading prices, completing a long questionnaire or providing an email address. Give them an easy first question, make the benefit clear early, and let them ask their own questions. Present signup after they have experienced the demo.

| Entry page | Suggested conversation focus | Example proposed opening |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Painting | How painting inquiries, measured job scope, owner-approved quoting and calendar booking fit together | “What kind of painting work do you mainly take on, and what happens to calls while you're on a job?” |
| Fencing | Gathering sufficient fence/gate details, applying the owner's configured prices and arranging the right booking outcome | “How do you currently handle new fencing inquiries when you're out working?” |
| Comparison | The visitor's buying criteria and the verified differences relevant to those criteria | “What matters most in your next system: quoting from your prices, booking jobs, or handling calls while you're busy?” |
| Pricing | Expected workload, included features, setup, plan fit and actual cost terms | “Tell me about your business and how many calls you handle, and I can explain which setup may suit you.” |
| Homepage | Discover the business type and its main operational problem | “What business do you run, and where do incoming calls cause the most extra work?” |

These are suggested sales openings, not deployed copy. The demo should explain how the complete operator uses the owner's approved prices and permitted calendar availability. Keep its product knowledge aligned with the features actually released. Clearly label any illustrative example as a demonstration. A working sales conversation does not constitute acceptance of the production phone operator.

The sales-demo branch already provides a relevant implementation starting point. Its live-provider/browser acceptance and publishing integration remain separate work. This proposal does not change Claude's ownership of the production voice and ON/OFF work, or authorize application changes.

**Trade pages: make the owner recognize their own business.** Start with painting, fencing and flooring pages connected to the matching demo context. This prioritizes acquisition content while preserving the intended product scope across Canada and the USA.

Each page should answer practical buying questions: what information is needed to quote accurately; how the approved book governs pricing; which calendar outcomes the owner can allow; what happens when details are missing; and how owners retain control. Use trade-specific examples and language rather than changing only the industry name on the same article. Proposed headline: “Handle painting inquiries while you're on the job.” Follow it with the concrete quote-and-book benefit once verified.

Measure demo starts and signups separately for each trade. Invest in the pages whose visitors become suitable activated customers. Google recommends useful content for the intended audience, including material that demonstrates relevant experience: [Google’s people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content). Once permission-based customer evidence exists, add actual onboarding and job outcomes.

**Comparison pages: help the visitor make a decision.** The existing alternatives/comparison library is a useful acquisition asset. Each page should explain the actual buying differences: owner-controlled quoting, booking workflow, website widget, setup, phone routing/control, supported integrations and comparable cost terms. Describe competitor capabilities using current linked sources; describe our capabilities using the verified release. Make limitations and fit clear.

Give each comparison page a contextual Talk to Off The Clock CTA. The demo can begin with the relevant buying criteria and tailor the explanation to the visitor's business. It must use verified competitor information rather than unsupported negative claims. Google recommends original research, supporting evidence and the decision factors that matter to buyers: [Google’s review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews).

Refresh the existing facts before publishing and connect the pages to the real demo/signup path. Indexing and canonical URLs belong to the approved publishing setup; the present review pages are not yet a verified acquisition funnel. Search traffic becomes useful when the visitor has a compelling next step and then completes onboarding.

**Agency partnerships: give another business a reason to introduce owners.** Target small website and marketing agencies already serving service businesses in Canada and the USA. Offer a clear package: a short voice demo they can show a client, a tested widget installation guide, an owner onboarding checklist and a concise explanation of quoting/booking value.

The agency could introduce the owner, help install the widget with permission and receive an agreed referral reward after the referred customer becomes paid. Set the reward after checking actual margins, including voice and AI costs. Start with a simple referral arrangement and owner-held accounts. An agency management portal can follow demonstrated partner demand.

A few active partners who introduce suitable owners may be more useful than a large unactioned partner directory. Proposed pilot: approach three to five relevant agencies after the product and installation journey are ready, then measure introductions, demonstrations, paid activation and support effort. No introductions or messages are authorized or sent by this proposal.

**First-customer proof: convert the demo's interest into confidence.** After the voice conversation has interested an owner, guide the first customers through actual business setup. That is the appropriate point to configure or organize their prices. Walk through representative jobs, inspect quote results, confirm calendar outcomes and resolve onboarding friction.

With permission, publish specific customer stories using actual observations: business type, setup time, relevant inquiry example, confirmed outcome and the owner's experience. Preserve customer privacy and the project's transcript-only/no-call-audio-storage rules. Marketing evidence can use approved screenshots, written quotes and independently produced explanatory material. Do not invent savings, testimonials or completed bookings.

**Conversion measurements.** Track entry page → demo opened → conversation started → signup clicked → account created → approved book published → first successful quote/booked job. These stages identify whether the problem is page relevance, demo usefulness, signup friction or activation. Start with these basic events before expanding analytics.

Optional test after the demo is reliable: compare Talk to Off The Clock against Hear how it works for your business. Evaluate both signup and activation outcomes. A higher click rate alone does not establish that a message brings better customers.

**Product improvements that support acquisition.** Prioritize a clear onboarding checklist, help with price-book units and inclusion rules, explicit USD/CAD context and an owner readiness check that completes a real quote-to-calendar journey. These improve the transition from an engaging sales conversation to successful product use.

**Suggested order.** Complete the core operator/routing/recovery/fulfillment gates. Verify and connect the shared sales voice demo. Connect the existing high-intent comparison pages and the first trade pages to it. Conduct assisted first-customer setups, collect permission-based proof and then test a small agency referral effort. Further expansion should follow measured activation and retention.

This is a proposal, not a commitment to untested marketing results or a change to the product's launch requirements.
