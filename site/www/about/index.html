About

An AI employee built for the trades

Off The Clock AI is a business in New Brunswick, Canada, serving service businesses across Canada and the United States.

Start free trialHear the live demo

Why it exists

Contractors lose jobs to missed calls and slow quotes. The calls come while you’re on a roof, under a sink or asleep, and the customer calls the next number on the list.

Off The Clock answers every call you send it, prices the job from your own price book while the caller is still on the line, and books the visit. On your website, the QuoteDone widget does the same for visitors who’d rather type.

How we build it

Facts only

Your AI employee says only what you’ve told it. When it doesn’t know, it takes the details for you instead of making something up.

A real price, from your numbers

Quotes come from your own price book. Jobs it can’t price from what the caller gives it come to you as a lead, never a guess.

You see everything

Every call transcribed and summarized, with email alerts. You always know what was said and what was quoted.

Keep what works

Keep your number. Keep your job software. Turn answering on and off from your own phone.

Prices in the open

Plans are on the website, setup is self-serve, and there’s no setup fee.

Your data stays yours

No business can see another’s records. No texts sent to your customers. No training AI on your calls.
