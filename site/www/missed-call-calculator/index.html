How this works

Four steps of arithmetic. Check it against your own figures.

  1. Weeks to months. Your missed calls per week times 4.33. A year is 52 weeks, and 52 divided by 12 is 4.33.
  2. Calls to jobs. Multiply by your close rate. Not every missed call would have become a job, so only the share you close when you do answer is counted.
  3. Jobs to dollars. Multiply the lost jobs by your average job value. Yearly is that figure times 12.
  4. Against the plan price. The last line divides your monthly figure by $279, the QuoteDone plan price. That is the only place our pricing enters the math.

The jobs figure is rounded for display, which is why it carries a ≈ sign; the dollar figures use the unrounded number. Revenue is not profit, some callers ring twice, and a few were never going to buy, so read the result as a top-line figure, not a bank statement.

The starting values are only a place to begin. Move all three to your own figures and the result is your arithmetic, not ours.

Questions

What counts as a missed call?

Any call that rang out, went to voicemail, or got picked up too late to be useful. If nobody spoke to the person, count it.

Why 4.33 weeks in a month?

52 weeks divided by 12 months is 4.33. Using 4 weeks would undercount you by about 8%.

Does the page use any industry statistics?

No. The only numbers here are the three you set and our own published pricing.
