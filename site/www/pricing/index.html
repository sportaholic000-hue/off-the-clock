Which plan fits

Starter

You want every call answered and every lead captured, with answers from your business info and the prices you list.

Operator

You want visits booked onto your calendar, callers transferred to you when you’re available, and a website that quotes and books.

QuoteDone

You quote measured jobs (roofing, painting, flooring, fencing, concrete, landscaping or siding) and want callers to hear a real range on the first call.

The details

Minutes
Talk time on your line. Starter 150, Operator 300, QuoteDone 1,200 each billing month. Unused minutes don’t carry over.
Usage warnings
The meter is always in your dashboard. Email at 60, 30 and 0 minutes left.
Overage
$0.35 per minute past your plan, charged each time it reaches $25 and at the end of the billing month.
Not counted
Calls that drop because the receptionist failed, and calls from numbers you’ve marked as spam.
Annual billing
10× the monthly price, so two months are free. Charged up front; minutes reset every month.
Free trial
14 days on your plan’s full features, up to 60 minutes of answered calls. Card required. For businesses that haven’t paid for Off The Clock before.
Setup
Self-serve. No setup fee.
Your number
Keep it. Forward calls with your carrier’s code; turn answering on or off from your phone.
Cancelling
Anytime from billing settings. Service runs to the end of the paid period. No partial refunds; final-month overage is still charged.
Failed payment
Answering continues 3 days while you update your card.
Your records
Download leads, quote requests and bookings as CSV anytime, and for 90 days after service ends.
Currency and taxes
Prices are in US dollars and don’t include taxes. Where tax applies, it’s added.
