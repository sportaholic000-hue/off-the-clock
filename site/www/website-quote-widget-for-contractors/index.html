Website quote widget for contractors

Visitors who land on your site at night want a number before they call. The QuoteDone widget gives them one, from your own prices, and lets them book.

Start free trialHear the live demo

What visitors get

  • The same questions your AI employee asks on the phone, for the service they pick
  • A quote range from your own price book
  • A time to book: with Google Calendar connected, open times from your calendar; otherwise the visitor gives a preferred time for you to confirm

Each request lands in your dashboard as a lead, with the answers the visitor gave.

Which plans include it

Operator ($119 a month) and QuoteDone ($279 a month). Both include a 14-day free trial.

Installing it

  1. Save your website’s exact address as an allowed website origin. The widget only runs on addresses you allow.
  2. Copy the widget code and paste it into your site.
  3. Publish, then use the installation check and try a full estimate.

No access to your website builder? Share the customer quote link from the app instead. Full steps in the widget guide.

Phone and website, one price book

On QuoteDone, callers and website visitors are quoted from the same prices, so nobody gets two different numbers. See how quoting works.
