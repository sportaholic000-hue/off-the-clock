Calls, transcripts and alerts

What you see after every call.

Every call has a transcript and summary in your dashboard. You get email alerts for new leads and callback requests, and quotes show the range and options the caller heard.

Transcription notice

You decide whether your AI employee tells callers the call is transcribed. It’s a setting in your account, off or on. Some places require callers to be told; you’re responsible for the laws that apply to your business.

Spam

Mark a number as spam and its future calls are rejected. Those calls aren’t counted against your minutes.

Returning callers

When someone calls back from a number that’s called before, your AI employee confirms who it’s speaking with before using anything from earlier calls.
