Connect your number

Keep your number and forward calls with your carrier’s code.

You keep the number on your trucks and cards. Off The Clock gives you a receptionist number behind the scenes, and you forward calls to it from your own phone. Setup shows the exact codes with your receptionist number already filled in.

Choose which calls it answers

  • Missed calls only. Your phone rings first; if you’re busy or don’t pick up, your AI employee answers.
  • Every call. Your AI employee answers all calls.

Carriers covered in setup

United States: Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile. Canada: Bell, Rogers and TELUS. For other carriers, setup shows the standard mobile forwarding codes. If a code is rejected, use your phone’s call forwarding menu or contact your carrier.

Turning it on and off

Dial the forwarding code to turn answering on and the turn-off code to turn it off. Your line rings normally again as soon as forwarding is off.

AT&T says call forwarding overrides your voicemail and can only be changed from the phone itself.
