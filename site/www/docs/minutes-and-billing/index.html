Minutes and billing

Included minutes, overage, trial and cancelling.

Included minutes
Starter 150, Operator 300, QuoteDone 1,200 each billing month. Talk time on your line. Unused minutes don’t carry over.
Warnings
The meter is always in your dashboard. You get an email at 60, 30 and 0 minutes left.
Overage
$0.35 per minute past your plan.
Not counted
Calls that drop because the receptionist failed, and calls from numbers you’ve marked as spam.
Free trial
14 days, card required, up to 60 minutes of answered calls. Trial minutes are never billed as overage.
Annual plans
10× the monthly price, charged up front. Minutes still reset monthly.
Cancelling
Anytime. Service runs to the end of the paid period. Export your records for 90 days after service ends.
