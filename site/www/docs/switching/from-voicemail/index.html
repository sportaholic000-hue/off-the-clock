Switching from voicemail

Stop sending callers to a beep.

Voicemail takes a message. Your AI employee answers, answers questions, and on the right plan books the visit and prices the job.

  1. Start your trial and fill in your knowledge base: hours, service area, services, policies and FAQs.
  2. Forward missed calls only with your carrier’s code. Your phone still rings first; your AI employee picks up instead of voicemail.
  3. Read the transcripts and summaries for your first week, and add anything callers asked that your knowledge base didn’t cover.
