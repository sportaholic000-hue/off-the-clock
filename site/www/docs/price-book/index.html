Your price book

How prices are set, confirmed and used for quotes.

Your price book holds the prices your AI employee and website widget quote from. It’s included on Operator and QuoteDone. Phone quoting from it is on QuoteDone; the website widget quotes from it on both.

Three ways to fill it in

  • AI interview. Answer questions about how you price each service.
  • Import from your website. Give your website address and it reads the prices listed there.
  • Type them in. Enter or edit any value directly.

Nothing goes live unconfirmed

AI-suggested prices are placeholders. You review and confirm each value, and a service only starts quoting once its prices are complete and confirmed.

How a quote is built

Your AI employee asks the caller for the details that service needs, runs them through your prices and gives a range. If your price book doesn’t cover something the caller needs, or the caller can’t give a detail it needs, it captures everything as a lead for you instead of guessing.

Tiered options

Services can have options, like Good, Better and Best. Each option is quoted with its own range.
