The call that costs you jobs

A homeowner with a leak or a twenty-year-old roof calls three roofers on a Saturday. Two are on a roof. The third gives a number and books the inspection.

What your AI employee asks

The details your own pricing depends on, asked one at a time in plain words a homeowner can answer.

Roof replacement

Roof size, roofing type, pitch, stories, how many layers are coming off, how complex the roof is, and full or partial scope.

Roof repair

Type of repair, affected area, roofing type, pitch, stories and whether there’s an active leak.

Flat roof replacement

Roof area, membrane type, existing layers, how hard the roof is to reach, and residential or commercial.

Flat roof repair

Type of repair, affected area, membrane type, active leak and ponding water.

What a quote needs

Your confirmed prices and the caller’s details

With both, the caller gets a range from your price book, confirmed on site, and on Operator and QuoteDone the visit gets booked.

Either one missing

If a price isn’t entered and confirmed yet, or the caller can’t give a detail the calculation needs, your AI employee captures the job in full instead: their details, everything they told it, and the transcript, in your dashboard. Nothing gets filled with a guess.

Phone quoting is on QuoteDone. The QuoteDone website widget runs the same price book on Operator and QuoteDone, so a visitor at midnight gets the same range a caller gets.

Questions

What if the homeowner doesn’t know the roof size?

Your AI employee asks in terms a homeowner can answer, and a best guess is fine on the phone: the range is confirmed on site. If the caller can’t give what the calculation needs, the job comes to you as a lead with everything captured.

Will it quote low and cost me margin?

It quotes only from your confirmed prices. If a price is missing, that service doesn’t quote; the job comes to you as a lead. Ranges are given as confirmed on site.

What if the caller’s measurements are off?

The range reflects what they told it, which is why it’s a range and confirmed on site. On your website widget, customers have time to measure and type exact numbers.

Do I have to price every service before I start?

No. Answering works right away, and each service starts quoting once its prices are complete and confirmed.

Can callers reach me directly?

On Operator and QuoteDone, your AI employee transfers callers to you live during the hours you set. Outside those hours it saves a callback request with the caller’s own words.
