Questions

Can it quote a full system replacement?

If you list a set price for it, yes. If you don’t, it captures the caller’s details and what they need so you can follow up.

Can it handle after-hours calls?

It answers every call you forward to it, any hour. On Operator and QuoteDone it transfers callers to you during the hours you set; outside them it saves a callback request with the caller’s own words. It isn’t an emergency line and doesn’t contact emergency services.

Will callers know it’s AI?

It answers with your business name and its own name and doesn’t announce that it’s AI. If a caller asks whether it’s a real person, it says it’s a digital employee of your business; if they ask whether that means AI, it says yes. It never denies it.
