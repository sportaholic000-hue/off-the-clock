Questions

Which siding types does it quote?

The types you offer and price: vinyl, fiber cement, wood or metal.

Will it quote low and cost me margin?

It quotes only from your confirmed prices. If a price is missing, that service doesn’t quote; the job comes to you as a lead. Ranges are given as confirmed on site.

What if the caller’s measurements are off?

The range reflects what they told it, which is why it’s a range and confirmed on site. On your website widget, customers have time to measure and type exact numbers.

Do I have to price every service before I start?

No. Answering works right away, and each service starts quoting once its prices are complete and confirmed.

Can callers reach me directly?

On Operator and QuoteDone, your AI employee transfers callers to you live during the hours you set. Outside those hours it saves a callback request with the caller’s own words.

What does it cost?

Phone quoting from your price book is on QuoteDone, $279 a month for 1,200 minutes. The website quote widget is on Operator ($119) and QuoteDone. Answering starts at $69 with Starter.
