Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Off The Clock AI ("we", "us") is a business in New Brunswick, Canada. We provide an AI receptionist that answers phone calls for service businesses, gives prices from each business's own price list, books appointments and runs a quote widget on business websites. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and what choices you have.

Questions or requests: support@offtheclockai.com.

Who this policy covers

  • Businesses that use Off The Clock, including the owner and any office staff they invite.
  • People who call those businesses or use a business's website quote widget. For these people, the business decides how the service is used and is responsible for its relationship with them. We process their information on the business's behalf.
  • Visitors to offtheclockai.com, including people who try the live demo.

Information we collect

From businesses

  • Account details: first name, business name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Business setup: business type, country and region, time zone, business phone number, the details you enter for your receptionist (about, hours, services, policies, answers to common questions, your listed prices), your price book, greeting, voice choice and transfer settings.
  • Calendar connection, if you connect Google Calendar: access limited to events on calendars you own and your free or busy times. The access credentials are stored encrypted.
  • Billing records: plan, subscription, invoices, payment status and minutes used. Card details are entered with Stripe and are not stored by us.

From calls and the website widget

  • The caller's phone number and the time and length of the call.
  • A written transcript of the call. We do not store call audio. Audio passes through our phone and AI providers while the call is in progress so the receptionist can listen and speak.
  • Details a caller or widget visitor gives: name, address, email, the job they need, measurements, quotes given and appointments booked.
  • When a caller asks for a copy of their quote by email, their email address and the quote we send.

From our website and live demo

  • Live demo conversations are sent to our AI provider as they happen so the demo agent can reply. We do not save demo conversations.
  • To limit how many demo sessions each visitor can start, we keep a one-way coded version of the visitor's IP address with the session time. These records are deleted after 2 days.
  • Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Cookies and browser storage

Signing in to the business app sets one cookie that keeps you signed in and stores a sign-in token in your browser. The website quote widget keeps an unfinished booking in the browser's session storage until the tab is closed. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use information

  • To answer calls, give prices from the business's own price list, capture leads, book appointments and run the quote widget.
  • To tell the business what happened on every call: transcripts, call details and email alerts.
  • To email a caller a copy of their quote when they ask for one.
  • To bill for the service, prevent abuse and keep accounts secure.
  • To answer support requests.

We do not sell personal information. We do not send text messages. Each business's information is kept separate, and no business can see another business's records.

AI processing

The receptionist's voice conversations, and features such as drafting business details and price suggestions, use Google's Gemini API on a paid plan. Google states that on paid plans it does not use prompts or responses to improve its products, and that it logs them for a limited period only to detect and prevent policy violations. We do not use your information to train AI models.

Service providers we use

  • Twilio: phone numbers and connecting calls.
  • Google: the AI that runs conversations (Gemini API), Google Calendar if you connect it, and website fonts.
  • Stripe: subscriptions and payments.
  • Resend: sending account, alert and quote emails.
  • Railway: hosting the service and storing encrypted backups.

These providers process information in the United States and may process it in other countries where they operate.

How long we keep information

  • While a business's service is active, we keep its records so the owner can review calls, leads, quotes and appointments.
  • Routine backups rotate and are kept for about 30 days.
  • After a business's service ends, the business can export its records for 90 days. The receptionist phone number is released 30 days after service ends.
  • 90 days after service ends, we delete the business's records: profile, calls and transcripts, leads, customers, quotes, appointments, price book, staff access and saved settings, including the copies in our managed backups. We also revoke any Google Calendar access.
  • We keep billing records, such as subscriptions, invoices and payments, after deletion. We also keep limited records of the deletion itself, such as the account identifier and deletion dates, so deleted information is not restored from an older backup.

How we protect information

Information travels over encrypted connections. Passwords are hashed, calendar credentials and backup copies are encrypted, and every request is checked against the signed-in business so one business cannot reach another's records.

Your choices

  • Businesses can review and export their calls, leads and quotes in the app, correct their details, and cancel at any time. Email us to ask for access, correction or deletion.
  • Callers and widget visitors should contact the business they called first, because the business controls those records. You can also email us and we will help the business respond.

Children

Off The Clock is a service for businesses. It is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will update this page and email active business accounts before the change takes effect.
