The difference

A price on the job, during the call

On the QuoteDone plan, your AI employee prices measured jobs during the call from your own price book (for example 120 feet of six-foot cedar privacy fence with one walk gate) and reads the caller a range. Jobs it can’t price from what the caller gives it become a lead for you instead of a guess.

On Operator and QuoteDone, the QuoteDone website widget gives visitors a quote range from your price book and lets them book a time.
