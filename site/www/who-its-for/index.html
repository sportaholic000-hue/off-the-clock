Who it’s for

For service businesses where answering the call is only half the job

Off The Clock is built for owners who are still the receptionist, pricing department and scheduler.

Start free trialHear the live demo

Best fit

The owner still handles most price questions

Customers call because they want to know two things: what the job will cost and when it can be done. Off The Clock puts the repeatable part of that knowledge into your AI employee.

The business has both fixed and variable services

A lawn-mowing visit, diagnostic fee or maintenance service may have a straightforward price. A fence, driveway, roof, floor or painting project needs more information. Off The Clock handles both workflows without forcing every service into the same script.

The company wants to keep its current tools

Off The Clock connects to Google Calendar and sends data through webhooks and CSV. The business does not have to abandon Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks or another system of record.

Website visitors should receive a price too

The QuoteDone website widget, on Operator and QuoteDone, gives visitors a price using the same approved pricing as the phone.
